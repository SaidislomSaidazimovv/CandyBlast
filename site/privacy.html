Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026.

Information we use

Candy Blast uses account information supplied by Google, Apple or email sign-in to identify your account. We store your display name, email association and game progress, including levels, scores, stars, lives, boosters, rewards and settings.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, restore progress across devices, protect account data and operate game features. We do not request access to Google Drive, contacts, calendars or other unrelated Google services.

Storage and deletion

Account and progress data is stored with Supabase. Guest progress remains on the device. Account deletion controls will be added before the public mobile release. Until then, deletion requests can be sent to the support email shown on the official store listing.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the mobile release, analytics and support systems are finalized.
